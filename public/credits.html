A little credit where it’s due.

This is an independent website concept by Nilesh, created to explore a possible direction for Spirkz. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Spirkz, NASA, NOAA, or the photographers. The sample lessons and interface are original demonstrations, not recordings of the live app.

Photography

  • Moon: NASA/JPL/USGS, Earth’s Moon, PIA00405. Galileo spacecraft imagery in enhanced color. Used according to NASA’s media guidelines.
  • Ocean: Sven Piek on Unsplash.
  • Fern: Aaron Burden on Unsplash.

The ocean and fern photographs are used under the Unsplash License. All three images are served locally; the browser does not contact the image providers.

Lesson references

Narrated video lessons were written for this concept. Their factual references are:

  • NASA — Moon facts
  • NOAA — Why is the ocean blue?
  • National Geographic — Photosynthesis

Type and icons

The three 32-second videos were animated and rendered locally with Remotion. English narration uses ElevenLabs George with the Multilingual v2 model, generated on the free plan for this local preview. This audio does not include commercial usage rights; regenerate under an appropriate license before publishing a promotional site. Diagrams are simplified illustrations; Earth and Moon distances and sizes are not to scale. Subtitles are provided in seven languages. English timing uses the voice provider's alignment; translated phrase timing is approximate. Visual labels and speech remain English.

Manrope is served locally under the SIL Open Font License. Interface icons are from Phosphor Icons.

Spirkz branding and languages

Language flags are served locally from flag-icons, under its MIT license.

The official owl app icon comes from the Spirkz Google Play listing. The owl mark comes from the official Spirkz website. Spirkz retains its branding rights. The seven language options match the original website; the concept copy and its translations were prepared for this demo.

Privacy

This concept has no signup form, accounts, analytics, cookies, or persistent learner tracking. Lesson progress lasts only while a preview is open. Your chosen language is saved in this browser's local storage and reflected in the page URL. The Android download and iOS update links take you to third-party websites, where their own terms and privacy policies apply.
